# Iliad MD v0.8.0

Keep a draft, outline, and notes open together. Opening a second document shows
tabs at the top of the window. Switching preserves each document's cursor,
scroll position, and undo history. Open documents also have a small ring in the
file tree, and Iliad restores them when you reopen the folder.

With one document open, its path stays at the top, including when the sidebar
is hidden or you are reviewing changes. Closing back to one document restores
that path. Tabs belong to their folder; different folders keep separate sets.

Press ⌘B for bold and ⌘I for italics, or Ctrl on Windows/Linux. The shortcuts
toggle ordinary Markdown formatting and work with selected text or the cursor.

Click placement around hidden formatting follows the surrounding space. Clicking
before a formatted word without a preceding space puts the cursor outside its
markers. With a preceding space, the cursor goes inside the markers. Clicking
immediately after the word keeps the cursor inside; clicking after the following
space puts it outside.

This release is signed and notarized for Apple Silicon Macs.
