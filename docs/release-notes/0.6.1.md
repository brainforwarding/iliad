# Iliad MD v0.6.1

Iliad MD v0.6.1 is a signed and notarized macOS release for Apple Silicon Macs. It's the first version that reaches you through the Update button.

## What's New

### Plainer AI writing

Suggestions and ✦ AI edits now follow a short set of writing rules.

- No filler, no obvious explanations and no empty repetition.
- Concrete details and direct verbs, when your document has them.
- No invented facts, numbers, sources or reasons.
- No generic openings, upbeat closing lines, inflated words like "crucial", or "not just X but Y".
- No em dashes or semicolons unless your own writing uses them.

Your voice comes first. A novel can still set a scene, and Spanish dialogue keeps its dashes. If you prefer a different style, say so in Settings → Writing and your preferences win.

Full idea suggestions (⌘/) are now shorter and denser, usually one or two paragraphs.
