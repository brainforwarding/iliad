Flow palette · direction study

Aqua is the water. Gold is the light.

The clean cream canvas stays. Cool aqua becomes the medium you write in — and because it passes contrast, it carries every interactive thing (links, active file, icons). Gold is reserved for light catching the surface: the paperclip mark, one call-to-action, and the shimmer in the agent's glow.

Calm, cool, fluid — light dancing on still water. That's flow.

The light on water

Generative caustics — broad swells, fine light veins, drifting iridescent streaks, sparkle nodes. Aqua-dominant; the warm glints are a minority, the way the photo is. In the app this only ever appears as a slow, faint shimmer (agent glow, the mark), never as a filled surface.

On flow

You write best when the tool disappears under your eye. The page is warm and still; the cursor moves like a current, and the only color that ever reaches you is the cool of the water and, now and then, a glint of light on the surface.

Agent is reading your selection…

CTA = gold (light). Everyday actions = water. Text accents never use bright gold (fails contrast on cream) — they use water-ink or gold-ink.
